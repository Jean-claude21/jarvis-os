# 065 — Offers on a board, CVs in the app

- A `kanban` card may say a `detail`, show a `badge` (a figure as a pill) and a `link` (a web
  address that opens in a new tab; the rest of the card opens the entry). Columns named in `muted` are
  faded, their titles struck through.
- Candidatures: figures (new on AI, to look into, CVs ready, applications), the last collection and its
  state, the new offers to tick, a board of AI offers by state (score, company, link), and « CV »:
  a master CV, layout templates and CVs tailored to an offer (state draft, ready, sent; an AI appraisal
  and review, for her criteria to come). Sources show their ready-to-open link.
