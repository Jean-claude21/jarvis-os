# 066 — A grid to appraise CVs

Candidatures gains « Criterion » (weight, what the AI checks, order) and shows her grid. The role tells
the assistant, for each offer: read it, learn about the company, write a tailored CV inventing nothing,
score it out of 100 with her criteria (appraisal + review per criterion), and make its PDF at 80 or more.
Offers seen on several sites are kept once by the Atelier (same title words, same company).
