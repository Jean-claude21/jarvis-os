# 068 — A map, types, offers known again, filters that offer their values

- Views: a filter « is » / « is not » on a text field chooses among the values the kind holds
  (`values` of a view result); « contains » suggests them.
- Maps: entries at one place make one pin (its size their number), coloured by the view's `by`
  choice, with a legend; its card lists them, each opening its entry and its web page.
- `receive` may match on several fields, tried in order (`["empreinte", "url"]`).
- Candidatures: offers gain a type (internship, work-study, CDI, other), a position and a fingerprint
  (from the Atelier); pages Today, Tracking, CVs, Settings, filtered by type; a map of the offers.
