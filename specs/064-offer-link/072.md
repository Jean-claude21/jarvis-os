# 072 — The offer's whole content, for the AI to read

- A rule's `include` may keep only the entries a formula keeps: `{ name: { kind, where?, limit? } }`.
- The Atelier's `lire` reads pages whole, without AI, and returns each page's main content in one
  field (« contenu »), matched by address.
- Candidatures: an offer gains « contenu » (the page's full text), « adéquation » (0–100, by the AI)
  and « avis de l'IA ». At 7:30, « Lire les offres » reads the kept offers (score 100, not set
  aside) that have no content yet; a page that cannot be read is said so, dated, and not asked again.
