# 076 — The side card, and no coloured bar on the left

## Why

The author asked to follow Gr8r Studio: an entry opens beside the page, not instead of it. And,
seeing the new agenda: « Enlève les bordures à gauche. Plus jamais. »

## What changes

1. **No coloured bar on the left, anywhere.** Agenda cards and chips, the selected item of a review
   list, the current line of a template, the line of now, quotes in documents: colour is shown by a
   small dot or a soft tint instead.
2. **The side card.** A click on any entry — an agenda card, a link, a table row — opens it in a
   card beside the page (560 px; full width on a phone):
   - its kind and space; its title;
   - its fields with their labels, times first, values in her words, corrected in place (« said »);
   - a web address as a link that opens the site;
   - its long texts (an offer's content, a note) folded, « Afficher tout » to read them;
   - what it is linked to (opened in the same card);
   - « Notes » and « Activité » as tabs; a note added from there;
   - at the top: open the site, copy the link, open the full page, close.
     Ctrl, ⌘ or Shift with the click still opens the full page; « Fiche complète » buttons too.

## Proof

Checked in the browser with a real week and two offers: an agenda card and a table row open the
card; the page behind stays; Escape and the scrim close it; at 390 px it fills the screen.
