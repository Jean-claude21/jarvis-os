# 078 — « Maintenant », as a home

## Why

Seen on 16 Nov: « Maintenant » showed a list, the record of one item, an empty day band and three big
cards « C'est fait / Demain / Annuler » for three buttons. The author asks to follow Gr8r Studio's
home: figures first, what waits in tabs, deadlines grouped by day, recent activity.

## What changes

1. **Head**: a greeting by the hour and her first name, today's date; « Ouvrir l'agenda ».
2. **Four figures**: À corriger, En retard, Aujourd'hui, Proposé — each with a line under it; a
   click shows that group in the list below.
3. **« Ce qui t'attend »**, three tabs:
   - **À venir**: today and this week;
   - **En retard**: what is late and what must be fixed;
   - **Proposé**: what Jarvis proposes and what she said without doing it.
     One line each: a round box that marks it done, its title, its kind and space, when, on the right.
     Its gestures (« Demain », « Annuler », « Accepter », « Planifier »…) appear on the line, small; a
     click on the line opens its side card (spec 076).
4. **Échéances**: what is dated today, tomorrow and this week, grouped under those three words, with
   « +N » beyond five per group.
5. **Ta journée**: today's timed things in hour order, only when there are some.
6. **Activité récente**: the last changes of her space, in a sentence and a time.

The same data as before (`/now`, `/history`); no coloured bar on the left.

## Proof

Checked at 1440, 1024 and 390 px with a real week: a box ticked marks the thing done (undone from the
toast); a tab and a figure change the list; a line opens the side card.
