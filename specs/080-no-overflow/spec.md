# 080 — Nothing overflows; what scrolls is framed

## Why

The author, 17 Nov, on « Activités »: the failures and the works ran down the page without end, their
long messages spread over many lines, and the field « Une chose à faire » stood 240 px high. « Il ne
faut pas qu'on déborde… qu'on soit encadré. »

## What changes

1. **No page scrolls sideways**; long words and addresses break inside their box.
2. **A long list stays in its frame**: a panel of more than five rows, the list of « Ce qui t'attend »
   past eight lines, recent activity, the list beside a record, the menus, the side card and the
   drawers scroll inside their own frame, with a soft shadow on the side where more is hidden.
3. **Thin scrollbars** in Jarvis's colours.
4. **A long message stays two lines** in a list; the whole of it is in its card.
5. **The add-a-task form is one line**: the field, the date, « Ajouter ».

## Proof

« Activités » with six failures of a long message, checked at 1440, 1024 and 630 px: the panels stop
at about 560 px and scroll inside; nothing passes the right edge of any page.
