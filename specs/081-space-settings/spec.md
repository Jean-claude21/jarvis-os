# 081 — « Réglages », and a space's own colour

## Why

The author, 17 Nov, showing Gr8r Studio's settings: a column of sections, the section beside it, and
per workspace its icon colour. In Jarvis a workspace is a space; its spaces stay on top (the switcher)
and at the bottom of the sidebar.

## What changes

1. **A space has an identity**: its name, its **colour** (a palette of eight or any other) and a line
   that says what it is for. An owner changes them; every change is journaled. The colour is the dot
   of the sidebar, the switcher, the agenda and the deadlines; without one, the order of her spaces
   gives one as before.
2. **« Réglages »** (sidebar, under her spaces, and the profile menu): sections on the left —
   Général (Compte, Apparence), Espaces (each of hers, « Créer, inviter »), Outils (Connexions,
   Modèles de type, Mes données), Historique (Journal) — and the section on the right.
3. **A space's section**: its icon in its colour and the palette; its name and line; its members;
   « Inviter quelqu'un »; its charter and routines; leaving a shared space.
4. **Apparence**: light, dark, or as the device says.

## Proof

A shared space « Nous deux » given green and a line: the sidebar, the switcher and the agenda show it
green; checked at 1440 and 390 px.
